Welcome to Navi Dish Swam Foundation

About Us

Navi Dish Swam Foundation is a non-profit organization dedicated to transforming lives through quality education, accessible healthcare, environmental sustainability, and livelihood opportunities.

Our Work

📚 Education

We run community learning centers and scholarship programs for underprivileged children.

🏥 Medical

We organize health camps, provide free medicines, and promote hygiene awareness.

🌱 Environment

We conduct tree plantation drives, cleanliness missions, and promote eco-awareness.

💼 Livelihood

We offer skill training and small business support to promote self-reliance.
