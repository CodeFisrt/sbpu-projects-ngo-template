About Us

Navi Disha Swam Foundation is a non-profit organization focused on community empowerment. We organize awareness campaigns, health check-ups, and educational drives to uplift marginalized sections of society.

Our Mission

Education for All

We provide free educational workshops and literacy drives for underprivileged children.

Healthcare Awareness

Organizing health checkups and AIDS awareness programs to build a healthier society.

Community Engagement

We host events and festivals to involve the community and spread joy with purpose.
