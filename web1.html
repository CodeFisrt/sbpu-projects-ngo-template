About Us

Who We Are

Navi Disha Swam Foundation is a non-profit organization committed to transforming lives through sustainable and impactful initiatives in education, healthcare, environment, and livelihood development. We are dedicated to uplifting communities by providing access to essential resources and opportunities for growth.

Our Mission

To uplift communities by addressing challenges in education, health, environment, and livelihood.
